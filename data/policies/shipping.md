# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
Delivery time is counted from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of $50.00 or more and costs $5.00 on smaller orders.
Express shipping costs $15.00 on every order.

## Order status
An order is "placed" until it is packed. It is then "shipped", and a tracking number is emailed
to the customer. After arrival it is "delivered". A cancelled order is never charged.

## Changes and cancellations
An order can be cancelled, or its delivery address changed, only while its status is "placed".
Once an order has shipped it cannot be changed. After delivery the customer can ask for a refund
under the refund policy.

## Lost packages
If an order has shown "shipped" for more than 10 business days without being delivered, the customer
should be handed to a human agent, who investigates with the carrier. The assistant cannot declare
a package lost.

## Where we ship
We deliver within the country only. International shipping is not available.
