# Warranty and Defective Items

## What is covered
Electronics are covered for 12 months from delivery against manufacturing defects.
Clothing and home items are covered for 90 days from delivery against defects in materials
or workmanship. Gift cards have no warranty.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, normal wear and tear, and damage caused by misuse or unauthorized repairs
are not covered.

## Reporting a defective item
Defect claims are decided by a human agent. The assistant does not judge a claim: it opens a
support ticket with the order number and what the customer reported. The human agent may ask the
customer for photos.

## Remedies
After review, a human agent can offer a repair, a replacement or a refund. A defect claim is separate
from the normal refund window: it can still be made after the refund window has passed, as long as it is
inside the warranty period.
